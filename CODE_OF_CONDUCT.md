# Community code of conduct

Treat everyone respectfully, regardless of identity, background, location, or
experience. Give specific, constructive feedback and focus discussion on the work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual content directed at others, deliberate
disruption, and disclosure of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Never publish employee records or private company information in community spaces.

Report concerns privately to [@krunvaghela](https://github.com/krunvaghela) using the
contact information on the maintainer's profile. Do not publish private evidence
in issues. Reports will be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed where disclosure is necessary to respond to a threat or legal obligation.

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict access
for violations. Responses should reflect the severity and repetition of behavior.
This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces. It does not create a guaranteed response-time commitment.
